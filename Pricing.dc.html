Your writing stays yours.
You retain ownership of scripts, notes, and material created in FilmScript. We do not sell your writing.
Lumiere is an assistant.
Lumiere offers editorial suggestions and analysis. You decide what belongs in your work and remain responsible for the final text.
Credits and limits.
Free includes a small set of Lumiere prompts, plus one AI script analysis, one AI breakdown, and one AI storyboard or shot list per account. Those free grants are account-based and are not restored by deleting a script. Creator includes FilmScript’s AI text tools and 100 image credits each monthly billing cycle. Full includes 1,000 image credits each monthly billing cycle. Every AI image generated in Imagine, Boards, or Shot List uses 3 credits. Unused image credits do not roll over and have no cash value.
Paid plans renew monthly.
Creator and Full renew through Recurrente. You can cancel or change your plan from your account menu. Access remains available according to the payment provider’s confirmed subscription status.
Fair use.
Do not use FilmScript to upload material you do not have permission to use, or to interfere with the service or other writers.
Questions?
Contact support if you need help with your account, billing, or your writing workspace.

Questions, answered.

Does Lumiere write my script?
No. Lumiere never adds a single line. It reads what you wrote and helps you make it sharper.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes. Move between Free, Creator, and Full at any time. Your existing scripts and production work stay available.
What do my pages export to?
Industry standard PDF, WGA checked, ready to send the moment you finish.
Which plan makes the production documents?
Free includes a limited AI introduction. Creator adds the AI text workflow. Full adds 1,000 monthly image credits for Imagine, Boards, and Shot List.
Is there a free trial?
Your first script is on us. Write it fully before you pick a plan.
